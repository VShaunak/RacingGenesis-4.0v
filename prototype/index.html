Onboarding & Playtest Guide

Welcome to Racing Genesis! You are evaluating our Feature Complete (v8.0) Formula 1 simulation. Please review the core mechanics below before your session:

🎮

Vehicle Driving Controls

W / ↑: Throttle acceleration (up to 228 mph)
S / ↓: Threshold braking & reverse
A / D (← / →): Progressive steering with inertia

🏎️

AI Autopilot

Choose AI Driver to watch the car complete laps, or Manual to drive it yourself.

📐

3D Circuit Sculpting

Key [: Open Track Editor
Toggle 3D Edit Handles: Click and drag cyan handles in the 3D scene to resize circuits from 1,000m to 10,000m in real time.

📊

Live Telemetry & Audio

Key ]: Open 90 Hz Telemetry oscillogram
Audio (M): Synthesized 8-speed V6 engine tone, curb rumble & tire scrub.
CSV Export: Download lap telemetry logs.

🎯 Think-Aloud Protocol: As you drive, please vocalize your thoughts aloud (what is clear, what feels intuitive, or where you get confused) so your observer can record your impressions!
